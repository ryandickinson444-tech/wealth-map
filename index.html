Life Stage Summary

Early on, build earning power. Over time, grow assets that can fund life beyond work. Tap any message to open that topic’s detailed guidance for the stage.

The full Life Stage Guides for every stage are included in the Map & Life Stage Guides PDF under Print & Download.

How to Use the Map

Choose a starting point, explore what matters to you, and flag topics to revisit.

  1. Choose a life stage. Pick the stage that best fits your situation. Ages are approximate, and more than one stage may be relevant.
  2. Choose a Major Wealth Driver, then a Key Part. Start with Money Sources, Money Uses, or Wealth Base, then choose a topic such as Work Income.
  3. Read the guidance. Explore the examples, assets and enablers, and action areas, then review Things to Do and Things to Avoid for your selected stage.
  4. Flag items for follow-up. Select an item’s flag control to mark something to learn about, discuss, or revisit. Select it again to remove the flag.
  5. Review your selections. Open My Flagged Items to review your list. Use Print & Download to print or save it.

You can switch stages while exploring a topic—just select the “Earlier” and “Later” buttons below the suggestions at the bottom. Earlier-stage guidance may still apply, and later-stage guidance can help you look ahead.
