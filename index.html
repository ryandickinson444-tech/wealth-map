Personal Wealth Map™

An InsightMaps framework

The Personal Wealth Map helps you use your resources and assets to fund your life and grow your wealth.

It breaks down the factors that drive wealth creation into three Major Wealth Drivers — Money Sources (how you create or obtain money and benefits), Money Uses (how you spend, give, save and invest money), and Wealth Base (what you build, own and harvest) — then breaks those down into the Sub Drivers behind them.

Here’s what to do:

  1. Choose your life stage at the top of the map.
  2. Choose a Major Wealth Driver, then choose a Sub Driver.
  3. Notice the Examples that explain what the Sub Driver means, the Strengthening Skills/Resources that help you make progress, and the Tips for things you might want to consider doing or avoiding at your life stage.

For the Examples, Skills/Resources and Tips, you can mark items (circles) as Needs improvement (tap once) or Priority (tap twice); tap a third time to clear. Your flags are saved on your device and can be printed as a one-page report (see the Print button at the top).

To print your Improvement Priorities Report and/or a copy of the complete Personal Wealth Map (8.5”x11”) choose the Print option at the top of the screen.
